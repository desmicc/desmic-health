A falsifiable research hypothesis · synthetic demonstrator

How little must we measure to understand enough?

Some channels share information; some reveal something no other channel can. This simulation trains a small regression model on 240 fictional participants and evaluates it on 120 different fictional participants. The results are calculated from invented data, not clinical performance.

01 / Select acquisition channels

Measurement subset

Costs are arbitrary. Motion and pulse share some information in this simulated world. Imaging is a reference channel, not a wearable sensor.

Model: regularized linear regression, standardized using training-only means and variances. Data split by participant; all samples synthetic.

What this demonstrates — and what it does not

Reducing cost is a constrained, target-specific optimization problem. A proxy can estimate an outcome only when the selected measurements contain relevant information; shared latent causes can create correlations without causation. Real studies must establish clinical targets, synchronized measurements, reference labels, independent validation, coverage, uncertainty, calibration, subgroup performance and safety. The results here deliberately encode these relationships; they do not establish real-world associations or replace MRI, EEG, laboratory tests or clinicians.
