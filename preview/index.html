Phase 0 / Three visual experiments

The life between appointments

Fictional narrative about Aarav; not a clinical product or evidence of device efficacy.

01 — A life like any other

Aarav makes tea before work. He has friends, ambitions, ordinary worries. Epilepsy is part of his life, not his identity.

02 — What happened between visits?

After an unwitnessed possible event, he cannot always reconstruct what happened. His clinician asks about symptoms, medication and sleep. The uncertainty belongs to the information gap, not to a failure by Aarav or his clinician.

03 — The missing story

Two visits are separated by 30 fictional days. The interval contains many experiences but only scattered records. Existing care can already use continuous monitoring; this is one fictional scenario.

04 — What if more could be seen?

A proposed wearable and patient-controlled journal could help connect observations, missingness and uncertainty for review. The clinician remains responsible for decisions. The proposal is not a seizure prediction guarantee or a replacement for medical testing.

Original research vision: Desmic (Kumar Shashank). These studies are concept experiments, not a finished website.
